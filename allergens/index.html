Allergens

Allergies, taken
seriously.

Every menu we cook is written for one event, so allergen information is written for it too.

Tell us early, and in full

Put every allergy and dietary requirement in your enquiry: who it is for, what they must avoid, and how severe it is. The earlier we know, the more we can plan around it.

What you get from us

Once your menu is agreed, we give you written allergen information for every dish, and we talk it through with you. On the day, ask us about any dish before you eat it — we will always answer.

The 14 major allergens

UK law names fourteen allergens that we must tell you about whenever they are in our food:

  • Celery
  • Cereals containing gluten, such as wheat, rye, barley and oats
  • Crustaceans, such as prawns and crab
  • Eggs
  • Fish
  • Lupin
  • Milk
  • Molluscs, such as mussels and squid
  • Mustard
  • Tree nuts, such as almonds, hazelnuts and cashews
  • Peanuts
  • Sesame
  • Soya
  • Sulphur dioxide and sulphites

What we can’t promise

Our food is prepared in a kitchen that handles many ingredients, so we can’t guarantee any dish is completely free from traces of an allergen. If an allergy is severe and we can’t cater for it safely, we will tell you plainly rather than take the risk.

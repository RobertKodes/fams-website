A family business

A family that cooks,
for other people’s occasions.

F.A.M.s started in 2021 as a family business, and it still is one. We cater weddings, private parties, corporate events and special occasions across Surrey and, by arrangement, further out.

Food is prepared fresh for the event, and the person you speak to when you enquire is one of the people who turns up on the day. There is no office, no account manager and no fixed package. You tell us about the occasion; we tell you what we would do with it, and what it would cost.

Our motto

Creating memories.

It’s written on the back of the van, and it’s the test we hold every job to. Nobody remembers the catering at a good event — they remember the day. The food is there to help that happen: on time, generous, and good enough that people go back for seconds.

A wedding, a fortieth, a christening, a team finally in one place: these are days people talk about years later. We would like to be a small part of why.

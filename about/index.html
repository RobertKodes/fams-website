About FAMs

Family is
at the heart
of everything.

FAMs is a family-run mobile catering company bringing freshly prepared food and warm service to events across Surrey and beyond.

Florin · Alexandra · Mirela · Sofia

Four names.
One family business.

Built around family, food and a genuine love of bringing people together, FAMs was created by Florin, Alexandra, Mirela and Sofia — with every event carrying a little piece of our family with it.

We believe great catering should be more than simply feeding your guests. It should become part of the experience.

From the moment we arrive to the last plate served, we focus on quality food, friendly service and attention to detail. Our food is prepared fresh and served with the warmth and personality you’d expect from a family business.

Who you’re dealing with

A family that cooks,
for other people’s occasions.

We cater across Surrey and, by arrangement, further out. Food is prepared fresh for the event, and the person you speak to when you enquire is one of the people who turns up on the day.

There is no office, no account manager and no fixed package. You tell us about the occasion; we tell you what we would do with it, and what it would cost.

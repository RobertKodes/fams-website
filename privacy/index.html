Privacy

Clear, simple
privacy information.

This page explains how enquiry information is intended to be handled. Final business contact details and hosting/form-provider details should be added before production launch.

Information you provide

When you submit the event enquiry form, you may provide your name, contact details, event date, location, guest numbers, dietary information and other details you choose to include.

Why the information is used

The information you submit is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss your event, prepare a quote and communicate about catering you have asked FAMs to consider.

Dietary and allergy information

Please only provide information that is relevant to planning your event. If you tell us about an allergy or dietary requirement, that information is used to discuss appropriate catering options with you.

Form processing and storage

The production site owner should identify the final hosting or form-processing provider here and state how long enquiry data is retained. The included website template is prepared for a Netlify-compatible static form, but that provider can be changed.

Your choices

You can ask what information has been provided through your enquiry and request that it is corrected or deleted where applicable. Add FAMs’ production contact email to this page before launch so privacy requests have a direct route.

Updates

This notice should be reviewed whenever the website’s contact tools, analytics, cookies or hosting arrangements change.

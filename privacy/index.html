Privacy and cookies

What happens to
what you send us.

Short version: we use it to answer your enquiry and run your booking, nothing else. This site sets no cookies and has no tracking. Last updated 13 October 2026.

Who we are

F.A.M.s Mobile Catering is a family business based in Surrey. We decide how the details you send us are used, which makes us the “controller” under UK data protection law. Contact: fams.company2021@gmail.com.

What you give us

Through the enquiry form: your name, email, phone number, company (if any), and the details of your event — type, date, times, guest numbers, location, the food you have in mind, allergies and dietary requirements, how you heard about us, and anything else you add.

If you message or email us directly, we receive whatever you choose to send.

Why we use it, and on what basis

To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if you book, plan and deliver the catering. The legal basis is that you have asked us to take these steps before entering into a contract, and then to perform it.

Allergy and dietary details can count as health information. We use them only to plan food that is safe for your guests, on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give with the tick box on the form. Please give us only what is relevant to the catering.

We do not sell your details, we do not use them for marketing, and we do not use them to make automated decisions about you.

Who else sees it

Netlify, Inc., which hosts this site and stores form submissions. Netlify is based in the United States and certified under the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which UK law recognises as an adequate safeguard for transfers to the US.

Google (Gmail), which receives the email copy of your enquiry.

WhatsApp (Meta). When you send the form, your details are written into a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing reaches WhatsApp unless you press send there yourself; if you do, WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to that message.

Beyond these, we share your details only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t become bookings are deleted within 12 months. For bookings, we keep what we need to deliver the event, and invoices and accounting records for as long as HMRC requires (normally six years).

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, delete it or stop using it, and withdraw your consent for allergy details at any time. Write to fams.company2021@gmail.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your details, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Fonts and images are served from this site itself, so visiting it does not send your details to Google or anyone else. If that ever changes, this page will change first.

Links to other sites

Our Instagram, Facebook and TikTok links open those platforms, which have their own privacy policies.

Booking terms

The small print,
in plain English.

These terms apply to every booking with F.A.M.s Mobile Catering. Your written quote sets out the details for your event; where it says something different from this page, the quote applies. Last updated 13 October 2026.

Who you are booking with

F.A.M.s Mobile Catering. You can reach us at fams.company2021@gmail.com. In these terms, “we” and “us” means F.A.M.s, and “you” means the person making the booking.

Quotes

Enquiries and quotes are free and don’t commit you to anything. Every menu is built for the event, so every quote is written for you: it sets out the menu, the number of guests, the serving times, the price, any deposit, the payment dates and how long the quote stays open.

Confirming a booking

Your date is booked once we have confirmed it to you in writing (by email or WhatsApp) and received any deposit stated in your quote. Until then, we can’t hold the date for you.

Changes to numbers, menu or timings

Tell us about changes as early as you can. We will always try to accommodate them, and we will tell you before the event if a change affects the price. Final guest numbers and any allergy details are needed by the date given in your quote, so that we can buy and prepare the right food.

Payment

Payments are due on the dates and by the methods set out in your quote or invoice.

If you need to cancel

Please tell us in writing as soon as you know. Any cancellation charges, and how much of a deposit can be returned, are set out in your quote; they reflect the food we have bought, the work already done, and other bookings we have turned away for your date.

Because our catering is booked for a specific date, the 14-day cancellation period in the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 does not apply to it. This does not affect your other legal rights.

If we need to cancel

We will only cancel for reasons outside our control, such as serious illness, the van breaking down or dangerous weather. If that happens we will tell you straight away, help you find an alternative where we can, and refund everything you have paid us for that event.

The venue and the van

You are responsible for making sure the van has permission to park at the venue, safe and suitable access, and a reasonably level space to work from, and for telling us about any rules the venue has. If the venue needs anything from us, such as insurance or food hygiene documents, let us know when you book.

Allergies and dietary requirements

Tell us about every allergy and dietary requirement in full, before the date in your quote. Once your menu is agreed we give you written information on the 14 major allergens for every dish. Our food is prepared in a kitchen that handles many ingredients, so we can’t promise any dish is completely free from traces of an allergen. How we handle allergens.

Food after service

Food is prepared to be eaten during the agreed serving times. For safety, we can’t be responsible for food kept and eaten after we have left.

Our responsibility to you

We will prepare and serve your food with reasonable care and skill, as the law requires. Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud, or for anything else the law does not allow us to limit. Otherwise, we are not responsible for losses that were not foreseeable when you booked, or that were caused by things outside our control.

If something isn’t right

Tell us on the day if you can, so that we can put it right there and then; otherwise, email fams.company2021@gmail.com as soon as possible after the event. We will look into it and reply properly.

The law that applies

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. If you live elsewhere in the UK, you can also bring proceedings in your local courts.

Questions about
the terms?

Ask when you enquire. We would rather explain them than have you guess.
